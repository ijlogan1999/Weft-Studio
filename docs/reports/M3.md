# Milestone 3 report: tracks and multitrack recording

## What works

- **Mixer graph.** The engine now has real channels:
  - audio, instrument and bus tracks become channels, processed so every channel runs before the buses it feeds;
  - fader, constant-power pan for mono tracks (−3 dB at centre) and balance for stereo ones;
  - pre- and post-fader sends, outputs to buses, and per-channel output and input meters;
  - mute silences a channel and its sends; solo keeps the soloed channels and everything up- and downstream of them (D-W6-1). Solo, record enable and the selected track are local view state (`LocalView`), never in the document.
- **Input monitoring** with Auto (armed and stopped, or recording), On and Off. Armed tracks meter their input while stopped.
- **Weft Keys**, the built-in instrument: 16 voices of three sine partials with a decaying envelope, sustain pedal, and all-notes-off. New instrument tracks get it by default.
- **Recording** (D-ARCH-18):
  - every armed audio track records its own input (mono or stereo, any input pair), from a lock-free FIFO to a writer thread;
  - files are Broadcast Wave at the project rate and bit depth (16, 24 or 32-bit float), written to `Recovery/` and moved to `Media/<hash>.wav` on stop, with BLAKE3 hashing;
  - one undo step "Record" adds the media, regions and take folders; recordings are named `<Track>#<n>`;
  - recordings shift by the driver's input and output latency plus a user offset, through the region's source offset (D-ARCH-19);
  - under 100 ms leaves nothing behind; a recording on a track that was deleted meanwhile lands on "<name> (recovered)";
  - cycle recording makes one take folder per track with a take per pass; the last take is active and `setActiveTake` switches;
  - autopunch records the punch range with 10 ms faded handles that crossfade with the material underneath (D-ARCH-20, D-ARCH-21);
  - R while recording ends the pass and keeps playing (D-W1-2).
- **MIDI.**
  - Live MIDI from `Engine::sendMidi`, sample-accurate by device frame, reaches the selected instrument track and every armed one, filtered by the track's MIDI channel (D-ARCH-22).
  - MIDI recording: notes, controllers, pitch bend, pressure and program changes with their channels; held notes close at stop and a held sustain pedal gets a release. Linear passes span whole bars; cycle passes merge into one region by default; linear passes replace by default and can merge into the region underneath.
  - The computer keyboard as a MIDI input (`KeyboardMidi`, D-W2-3).
- **Track commands.** New tracks with a count ("Audio 2", "Audio 3", "Audio 4" below the selected one, one undo step), Duplicate with or without regions (MIDI regions get their own copy of the notes), track and region colors, and the disk-space check behind the recording warning.
- **Disk streaming** (D-ARCH-17). Files longer than 30 s play from 32,768-frame blocks that a disk thread loads ahead of the playhead and the cycle start. A late block plays silence and counts a disk underrun. This replaces the in-memory media of M2 (D-ARCH-12).
- **Devices** report their input and output latency, and the null device can loop its output back into its input with a fixed round trip, which is how latency compensation is tested.

## Known issues

- **While recording, existing regions on the armed track keep playing** under the new recording (W-1a step 6 says the recording should cover them as it grows). The finished recording does cover them.
- **A locate to a part of a long file that isn't loaded can start with a few milliseconds of silence** until the disk thread catches up; the underrun counter shows it. Play and locate hint the disk thread, which keeps this short.
- **Live notes aren't tracked by the transport,** so changing the selected track while holding a key can leave that note hanging on the old instrument (its release goes to the new track).
- Carried over from M2: the ASIO SDK question (D-ARCH-14) and the app not using the engine yet (M4).
