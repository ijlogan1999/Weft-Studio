# Milestone 5 report: the piano roll

## What works

- **Piano roll** in the editors area (P, or double-click a MIDI region). It shows the opened region plus any other selected MIDI regions on the same track, with:
  - a keyboard (C3 = 60; clicking a key plays it and selects every note of that pitch, Shift adds);
  - its own ruler with bar numbers, region spans and the playhead (click or drag to locate);
  - a note grid with velocity-coded brightness, note names when wide enough, hollow muted notes, highlighted selection, shading outside the regions, and a faint mark at a note's stored position when region quantize moves it;
  - a controller lane: Velocity (default), Modulation CC 1, Sustain CC 64, Pitch Bend (with a centre line), Aftertouch, any CC 0–127, or hidden;
  - a toolbar with the tool (pointer, pencil, eraser, velocity, scissors), snap grid, quantize grid, strength, swing, the Q button, audition and follow toggles, and the lane selector.
- **Note editing.**
  - Pencil or Ctrl+click draws a note of the last-used length and velocity on the grid cell clicked; dragging on lengthens it in the same undo step.
  - Click, Shift+click, rubber band, piano keys and Ctrl+A select.
  - Drag moves (Alt without snap); Ctrl+drag copies; edges resize with snap, keeping the other edge.
  - Up/Down transpose, Shift for an octave; Left/Right move by the grid.
  - Delete or the eraser removes; Ctrl+X/C/V copy and paste at the playhead into the region there.
  - The velocity tool shifts the selection's velocities together, each clamped to 1–127; lane bars set one note; a pencil line across the lane ramps them.
  - Ctrl+M mutes and unmutes; scissors split a note.
  - Notes never start before their region; notes past the end grow the region to the end of that bar.
  - Every edit is one undo step.
- **Inspector.**
  - The note section shows pitch, velocity, length, position and channel for the selected notes. Typing a value sets every selected note; mixed values show "—", and mixed velocities show their range.
  - MIDI regions get Quantize (Off or a grid value), Strength, Swing and Apply Quantize.
- **Quantize.**
  - Q quantizes the selected notes, or every note in the shown regions, as one undo step "Quantize", with strength and swing per W-5.
  - Quantize is idempotent and stops at the region start.
  - Region quantize plays notes on the grid while their stored positions stay put. Apply Quantize bakes the positions and turns region quantize off, in one step.
- **Playback.**
  - Controller points play as CC, 14-bit pitch bend, channel pressure, poly pressure and program change, at their positions in each region (looped and split regions included).
  - Muted notes stay silent in playback and offline renders.
  - **Audition** sends a note straight to the track's instrument for its length, at most 1 s, through a lock-free queue; nothing on the audio thread locks or allocates.

## Stubbed or not yet in the piano roll

- AT-PRL-9 and AT-PRL-13 call for the `TestSynth` VST3 fixture. Plug-in hosting arrives in M6, so they run against the engine's probe instrument for now, which sounds at velocity/127 just like `TestSynth`. They'll be repointed in M6.
- Collaborator pointers and note selections wait for collaboration (M10–M11).
- There are no zoom sliders yet. Ctrl+wheel zooms time, Ctrl+Alt+wheel changes row height, and the wheel scrolls.
- Controller points can't be rubber-band selected or copied, and there's no right-click menu.
- v2 piano roll features (note-end quantize, scale tools, legato, humanize, event list, drum mode, ghost notes) aren't started, as planned.

## Known issues

- Windows UI tests run only on the Windows CI runner. Nothing here was clicked through by hand on Windows.
- Multi-region Apply Quantize from the Edit menu makes one undo step per region.
